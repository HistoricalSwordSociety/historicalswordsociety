Study · Preserve · Practice

Our Mission

To preserve and advance the knowledge of historical swordsmanship through education, research, and structured training—ensuring these traditions remain accessible to future generations.

We combine historical treatise analysis, the study of surviving swords, and hands-on instruction to understand the European sword and its legacy.
