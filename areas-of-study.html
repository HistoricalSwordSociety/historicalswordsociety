Research through many lenses

The sword is only the beginning.

Historical swordsmanship brings together the study of objects, written sources, materials, and the people who used them. Explore the Society’s six research disciplines, each offering a different way to understand European sword traditions.

01 / AREA OF STUDY

Historical European Weapons

Explore the evolution, design, and martial use of European weapons, from early swords to Renaissance rapiers and military sabres.

Weapon evolution · Design · Martial traditions

Primary sources · Fencing masters · Interpretation

03 / AREA OF STUDY

Sword Collecting & Artifact Analysis

Examine antique blades, hilts, markings, and construction details to understand the surviving sword as a source of historical evidence.

Identification · Markings · Construction

Metallurgy & Blade Construction

Investigate forging methods, steel composition, heat treatment, and blade geometry to understand how materials shape a sword’s performance.

Steel · Forging · Blade geometry

Historical Context & Cultural Impact

Discover the sword’s place in European society, including military service, dueling culture, artistic expression, and ceremonial traditions.

Military history · Dueling · Ceremony
